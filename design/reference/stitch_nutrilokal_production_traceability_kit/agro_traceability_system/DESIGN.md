## Brand & Style

The design system is crafted to empower rural cooperative members and agricultural operators—specifically women farmer groups handling local processing of commodities like cassava and aloe vera. The visual language balances warm, communal accessibility with operational precision. It avoids intimidating enterprise complexity in favor of grounded reassurance, tactile clarity, and high-legibility touchpoints.

The aesthetic fuses **Modern Organic Functionalism** with **Minimalist Utility**:
- **Tone & Demeanor**: Reliable, gentle, dignified, and direct. The interface speaks clearly without dense jargon, honoring local labor and food safety traceability.
- **Visual Texture**: Light, airy, and grounded by organic tones. The system eliminates stark blacks and aggressive tech-blue accents, utilizing deep green-charcoal and botanical tones that feel natural in village production halls, processing kitchens, and field log stations.
- **Operational Focus**: Large touch targets, immediate status cues, and forgiving layouts built for operators navigating inventory, batch tracking, and moisture control with varying levels of digital familiarity.

## Colors

The color architecture is derived from agricultural vitality, raw botanical harvests, and clean packaging standards. High contrast ratios ensure ease of operation under varied outdoor and indoor sunlight conditions.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#315C40`)**: Forest Green. Anchors primary actions, confirmed states, primary navigation indicators, and prominent metric callouts.
- **Secondary (`#6E9272`)**: Sage Green. Used for secondary interactive borders, supportive actions, secondary metadata badges, and active tab borders.
- **Tertiary (`#E8F0E6`)**: Light Sage Tint. Serves as container fills for highlighted metric tiles, active selection pills, badge chips, and soft focus states.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F7F8F3`)**: Warm Off-White/Cream. The base background for the overall application canvas, calming eye strain compared to harsh sterile whites.
- **Surface Clean (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure White. Dedicated to cards, modal sheets, tables, and layered input fields to create legible surface planes over the cream canvas.
- **Text Primary (`#24352A`)**: Deep Forest Charcoal. Provides optimal WCAG AAA readability for body content, headers, and dense data points without cold black dissonance.
- **Text Secondary (`#718071`)**: Muted Lichen. Reserved for batch timestamps, helper texts, units of measurement, and table column captions.
- **Stroke / Border (`#E0E7DE`)**: Soft Moss-Grey. Applied at 1px thickness to demarcate cards, structured cells, and inputs without heavy visual noise.

### Functional States
- **Success / Verified (`#2E7D47`)**: Confirmed harvest lots, passed quality-control assays, and healthy inventory counts.
- **Warning / Pending (`#D9822B`)**: Warm Amber. Expiring batches, moisture calibration cautions, and pending field logs.
- **Danger / Critical (`#C24838`)**: Muted Brick Red. Spoiled lots, failed seals, out-of-stock events, and destructive prompts.

## Typography

The type system pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines and interactive labels with **Inter** for dense transactional bodies, numerical tables, and input flows.

### Editorial Guidelines
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** brings rounded terminals and friendly openness, instilling confidence and approachability for community group members. Use it for page headers, card titles, key metric numbers, and button labels.
- **Inter** ensures crisp horizontal rhythm, tall x-height, and neutral structure for batch codes, dates, weight entries (kg, gr), and procedural production steps.
- **Data Legibility**: For batch tables, inventory balances, and unit prices, use tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to preserve column alignment.
- **Tone in Language**: When authoring copy in Bahasa Indonesia, keep phrasing affirmative, respectful, and concise (e.g., "Simpan Hasil Panen", "Verifikasi Batch", "Unduh Label QR").

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column grid system designed to support both tablet/desktop processing terminals and mobile devices used in processing sheds.

### Breakpoints & Grid Rules
- **Mobile (`< 640px`)**: 4-column layout with `1rem` (16px) margins and gutters. Elements span full width or pairwise (2 columns). Key production actions are anchored to sticky bottom sheets for one-hand operation.
- **Tablet (`640px - 1024px`)**: 8-column layout with `1.5rem` (24px) margins and gutters. Suited for workshop counters, allowing side-by-side verification forms and batch summaries.
- **Desktop (`> 1024px`)**: 12-column layout with `2rem` to `3rem` (32px to 48px) margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Maximum content canvas is constrained to `1280px` centered width.

### Spacing Cadence
Layouts follow an 8pt modular grid (`0.5rem`, `1rem`, `1.5rem`, `2rem`, `2.5rem`) with a `0.25rem` (4px) sub-unit for compact badge padding and form controls. Preserve spacious paddings around inputs and operational triggers to minimize missed taps during high-activity food preparation sessions.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established primarily through **tonal separation** and soft, natural ambient shadows rather than harsh drop-shadows or stark dark outlines.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base)**: `#F7F8F3`. The natural earth-cream ground.
- **Level 1 (Flat Cards & Structural Panels)**: `#FFFFFF` surface accompanied by a crisp `1px solid #E0E7DE` border. No drop shadow is required; the tonal shift against the canvas establishes crisp structure.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Cards & Tables)**: Used for batch status cards, hover states, and record items. Uses a soft organic shadow: `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px rgba(36, 53, 42, 0.06), 0 4px 12px rgba(36, 53, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Slide-overs & Sticky Bars)**: Reserved for QR code scanner overlays, batch creation sheets, and floating confirmation bars. Uses `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px rgba(36, 53, 42, 0.08), 0 2px 6px rgba(36, 53, 42, 0.04)`.

Shadows are tinted with the primary text tone (`rgba(36, 53, 42)`) rather than synthetic neutral black, preserving an authentic and harmonious field-to-table finish.

## Shapes

The interface implements **Roundedness Level 2**, delivering approachable, organic geometry that balances welcoming curves with industrial efficiency.

### Corner Radius Tokens
- **Standard (`0.5rem` / 8px)**: Applied to text input fields, table selection cells, secondary button groups, and batch item listings.
- **Large (`1rem` / 16px, `rounded-lg`)**: Applied to high-level cards, data metric panels, modal containers, and production summary widgets.
- **Extra Large (`1.5rem` / 24px, `rounded-xl`)**: Applied to mobile bottom sheets, floating utility toolbars, and hero promotional banners.
- **Pill / Circular (`9999px`)**: Reserved exclusively for status chips, batch verification tags, counter indicators, and avatar monograms.

## Components

- **Primary Action**: Background `#315C40`, text `#FFFFFF`, border `none`, radius `0.5rem` (8px). Minimum height `48px` to guarantee confident touch usability. Hover: `#284D35`. Active: `#1F3B29`. Focus-visible: 2px ring `#6E9272` with 2px offset.
- **Secondary Action**: Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#315C40`, border `1px solid #6E9272`, radius `0.5rem`. Hover: `#E8F0E6`.
- **Subtle / Ghost**: Background `transparent`, text `#24352A`. Hover: `#E8F0E6`.

### 2. Status Chips & Badges
- Display metadata like batch status, crop grade, and safety seals.
- Height: `28px` to `32px`, pill-shaped (`9999px` radius), padding `4px 12px`.
- **Success / Lolos Uji**: Background `#E8F0E6`, text `#2E7D47`, border `1px solid rgba(46, 125, 71, 0.2)`.
- **Warning / Menunggu**: Background `#FDF3E7`, text `#D9822B`, border `1px solid rgba(217, 130, 43, 0.25)`.
- **Alert / Karantina**: Background `#FCECE9`, text `#C24838`, border `1px solid rgba(194, 72, 56, 0.25)`.

### 3. Cards & Metric Tiles
- **Default Card**: Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E0E7DE`, corner radius `1rem` (16px), interior padding `1.25rem` (20px).
- **Stat Tile**: Top border accent `3px solid #315C40` or subtle `#E8F0E6` inner wash. Clearly emphasizes key metrics (e.g., "Total Olahan Singkong: 240 kg") with `display-lg` scale numbers.

### 4. Input Fields & Steppers
- Height `48px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E0E7DE`, corner radius `0.5rem`.
- Active / Focus: border `1.5px solid #315C40`, background `#FFFFFF`.
- Suffix units (`kg`, `pcs`, `°C`) set in `body-sm` with `#718071` background fill on trailing edge.
- Increment/Decrement Steppers: Tactile `+` and `–` tap targets (minimum 44x44px) flanking numerical weight fields for fast processing logs.

### 5. Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Box size: `20x20px`, corner radius `4px` (checkbox) or `9999px` (radio).
- Default: Border `1.5px solid #718071`, background `#FFFFFF`.
- Checked: Background `#315C40`, border `1.5px solid #315C40`, icon white checkmark.

### 6. Data Tables & Batch Lists
- Header: Background `#F7F8F3`, border-bottom `1px solid #E0E7DE`, typography `label-sm` in `#718071` uppercase with `0.04em` tracking.
- Row: Background `#FFFFFF`, height `56px`, hover state `#F7F8F3`.
- QR Code cell embeds high-contrast SVG preview with direct tap-to-print actions.
